Frequently asked questions

How do we connect our phone numbers?–
We provide a SIP URI that you can route calls to from your existing PBX or dialer (like Exotel or Ozonetel).
Can the agent pause when a customer interrupts?+
Yes. It handles barge-in natively, stopping mid-sentence when the customer starts speaking.
How do we prevent hallucinations?+
The agent uses strict guardrails and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) constrained to your knowledge base. It is instructed to say "I don't know" or hand off to a human for anything outside those bounds.
Can it speak regional languages?+
Currently, the agent supports English, Hindi, and Hinglish natively, with the ability to switch between them mid-sentence.
