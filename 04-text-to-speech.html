Frequently asked questions

How many voices are available?–
A range of male and female voices across different tones. See the voice gallery above.
Can I control pitch and pace?+
Yes — pitch, speed, and pause duration are all adjustable via the API.
What audio formats are supported?+
MP3, WAV, OGG, and raw PCM. Streaming returns audio in real-time chunks.
Streaming or REST — which should I use?+
Use streaming for real-time voice agents. Use REST for batch generation like IVR prompts or notifications.
Can we get a custom voice?+
Custom voice cloning is available on enterprise plans. Talk to sales to learn more.
