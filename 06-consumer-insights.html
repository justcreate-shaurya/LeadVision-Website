Frequently asked questions

Do I need to define topics in advance?–
No. Topics are detected automatically from the calls. You can also pin or rename topics once they appear.
How quickly do insights appear?+
Topics and sentiment for a call are available within minutes of the call ending.
How is sentiment measured?+
Sentiment is scored on a 1–5 scale per call, based on the full conversation — not individual words.
Can I ask it a question in plain language?+
Yes — type a question and get an answer grounded in your call data, with the source calls cited.
What languages are supported?+
Hindi, English, and Hinglish. The system handles code-switching within a single call.
